How results are graded: every pick locked here comes from the 7 Top Props categories (Passing/Rushing/Receiving Yards, Receptions, and the 3 TD Scorer lists) -- never a broader "all players with a line" pool. Picks lock in before that week's games are played, using whichever real side (Over or Under) the model actually favored at the time -- never re-picked with hindsight afterward. A pick only gets graded once the real game has actually been played and reported; nothing here is guessed (see the note below for how Weeks 2-4 were recovered). Rating Calibration below checks whether each rating bucket's real observed win rate matches what that rating claims (e.g. a real 7.0-7.9 pick should win roughly 70-79% of the time) -- a genuine test of whether ratings are honestly balanced, not just a record of wins and losses.
How Weeks 2-4 were recovered: a bug in the lock step meant only Week 1 was ever saved (Weeks 2 and 3 never locked; Week 4 locked under the wrong week number). Weeks 2-4 were rebuilt from the Top Props page exactly as it was published on each week's first full run, before kickoff, then graded against real box scores with the same rules as everything else. Week 2's page had no Over/Under labels, so the side was taken from the rating (below 5.0 = Under), and 4 coin-flip picks rated exactly 5.0 were left out. Players who did not play are not graded. Parlays for Weeks 2-3 were recovered the same way; Week 4's parlays were never published. Game Lines picks (moneyline, spread, over/under) for Weeks 1-4 were also rebuilt from the published Game Lines table and graded against real final scores; the Week 1-2 table showed both moneylines, so that moneyline pick is the team the page projected to score more. Picks lock automatically from Week 5 on, and Game Lines picks lock on every build from now on.
