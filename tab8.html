How results are graded: every pick locked here comes from the 7 Top Props categories (Passing/Rushing/Receiving Yards, Receptions, and the 3 TD Scorer lists) -- never a broader "all players with a line" pool. Picks lock in before that week's games are played, using whichever real side (Over or Under) the model actually favored at the time -- never re-picked with hindsight afterward. A pick only gets graded once the real game has actually been played and reported; nothing here is guessed or backfilled. Rating Calibration below checks whether each rating bucket's real observed win rate matches what that rating claims (e.g. a real 7.0-7.9 pick should win roughly 70-79% of the time) -- a genuine test of whether ratings are honestly balanced, not just a record of wins and losses.
